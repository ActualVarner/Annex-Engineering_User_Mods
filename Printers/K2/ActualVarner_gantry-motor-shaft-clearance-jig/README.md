# Clearance jig for K1/K2 r3 gantry

Note the gantry of both K1 and K2 is identical to r3 for r4 so this may be used for either R3 or R4 builds.

The gantry for r3 places the motors closer to the gantry extrusions, while also increasing shaft length so there can be a supporting bearing for the end of the shafts. This necessitates drilling clearance holes in your X/Y gantry extrusions.

The provided STL is a jig for the above purpose.

## Materials
- 3x Brass bushings you should already have (12mm OD, 8mm ID, re-use from drilling clearance holes for the machine's frame)
- 8mm drill bit you should already have (from aforementioned clearance holes)
- About 30g of PLA filament
- Clamp or a few M5 x whatever works mm bolts with roll-in nuts to hold it in place.

## Instructions
1. Print the thing with flat face down. Tolerances are tiny and probably require PLA. Sorry.
2. Insert bushings. It's a little thinner than the bushings, may require percussive persuasion. Ensure they are flush on the side which will contact the gantry.
3. MARK with SHARPIE the approximate spot of your motor shaft after TRIPLE CHECKING you have the correct corner printed part in place.
4. Place on your gantry, then secure with clamp and/or M5 bolts with roll-in nuts. The part assumes you have an on-spec gantry; if you have short overhangs for the Z drives by more than ~1.5 mm the holes may be wrong.
5. There are up to 8 spots for bolts, go as crazy as you want.
6. CHECK AGAIN before drilling that your mark matches which bushing you intend to drill through, and reference the associated part by placing it on top.
7. CHECK ONE MORE TIME. ONLY ONE is correct at any position. Do all 3 and you may critically weaken your gantry.
8. Drill the hole.
9. ...
10. Profit? Nah, not yet, move on and do the other 3.
